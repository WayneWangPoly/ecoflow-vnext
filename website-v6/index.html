Materials & sustainability

Know what your packaging is made from.

Sustainability should be practical, not vague. Explain the material, verified certification and correct end-of-life pathway.

01

Materials

Paper and fibre formats, PLA and other bio-based options, plus water-based barrier technologies selected for the actual use of the product.

02

Certifications

Verified marks and licence details belong beside the products they actually cover. This prototype deliberately leaves certification claims generic until documents are supplied.

03

End of Life

Compostable, recyclable and general-waste pathways are not interchangeable. Clear disposal guidance helps venues communicate the right action to staff and customers.
